# Announcement to staff

Ardent Vale Ltd
Ref: AV-COMMS-2022-41
Date: 19 October 2022
Circulation: all staff, all sites

## What has happened

Ardent Vale Ltd acquired Cendria Software Ltd in October 2022. The transaction was
signed on 14 October and completed five days later. No site closures are planned as
part of the transaction.

## What changes now

Nothing changes for anyone this week. Payroll, benefits and reporting lines stay as
they are until the integration plan is published in the new year.

## What we will say externally

The holding statement below has been agreed with counsel. Please do not add to it.

> "The transaction strengthens our position in industrial software. Both businesses
> continue to trade under their existing names for the time being."

## Questions

An open session is scheduled for 26 October at 15:00 in the Leeds room, dial-in
details to follow.
