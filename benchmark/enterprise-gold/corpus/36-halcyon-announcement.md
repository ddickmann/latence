# Announcement

Halcyon Verpackung GmbH
Ref: HV-ANN-2023-12
Date: 7 July 2023
Circulation: all employees

## Transaction

Halcyon Verpackung GmbH acquired Marnix Textil BV in July 2023. The purchase completed
on 5 July and the acquired business joins the group with immediate effect.

## Why

The acquisition secures a source of technical fabric that has been in short supply for
two seasons. It is not expected to change the product programme.

## Practical matters

Reporting lines, payroll and IT access remain unchanged for everyone until further
notice. An information session is planned for September.

## Questions

Questions go to the works office in writing. Answers will be collected and published
as a single note.

Management
