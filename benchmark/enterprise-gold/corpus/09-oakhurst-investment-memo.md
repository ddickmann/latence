# Investment committee memo

Oakhurst Capital Partners
Ref: OCP-IC-2022-27
Date: 3 November 2022
Committee: industrials
Status: approved

## 1. Position

Oakhurst Capital Partners invested in Pelmar Robotics SA in 2022. The position was
taken alongside the existing shareholders and carries one board observer seat. The
holding period assumed at entry was five years, unchanged from the paper approved by
the committee in September. Follow-on capacity was reserved at the same time and has
not been drawn. Nothing in this section constitutes a valuation statement; the
carrying value is set by the valuation cycle described in the quarterly letter.

## 2. Company profile

Pelmar Robotics SA is based in Porto. The company assembles handling systems for
mid-volume industrial customers and runs a small applications lab on the same site.

## 3. Management contact

Camille Aubertin works at Pelmar Robotics SA and is the committee's day-to-day contact
for reporting questions.

## 4. Reporting

Monthly management accounts are due on the tenth working day. The first full quarter
under the new reporting pack is Q1 2023.

## 5. Committee decision

Approved as presented. No conditions attached.
