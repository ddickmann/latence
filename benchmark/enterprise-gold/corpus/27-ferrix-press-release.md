# Press release

Ferrix Automation BV
Date: 5 February 2024
Ref: FA-PR-2024-02
For immediate release

## Transaction completed

Ferrix Automation BV acquired Nyquist Sensors Oy in February 2024. The purchase was
completed following clearance by the relevant competition authority.

## Rationale

The acquisition adds sensing capability to the existing controller range. No changes
to either product roadmap are planned in the current year.

## Employees

The acquired company keeps its site and its management team. Employment terms are
unaffected by the transaction.

## Note to editors

Neither party comments on the consideration paid. Further information is available
from the press office on request.
