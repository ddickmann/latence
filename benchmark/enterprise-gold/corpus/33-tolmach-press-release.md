# Press release

Tolmach Bergbau AG
Date: 30 May 2022
Ref: TB-PR-2022-06
Distribution: trade press, staff notice boards

## Acquisition

Tolmach Bergbau AG acquired Ravenport Terminals Ltd in May 2022. The transaction gives
the group direct access to deep-water handling capacity for the first time.

## Integration

The acquired business continues to operate under its own management. A joint planning
group will report on integration options within twelve months.

## Employment

No redundancies are planned in connection with the transaction. Existing terms and
collective agreements continue to apply at the acquired site.

## Contact

Trade press enquiries to the communications office; no interviews are being given
this week.
