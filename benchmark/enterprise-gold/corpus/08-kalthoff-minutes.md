# Minutes - manufacturing review

Kalthoff AG
Meeting ref: KA-MR-2024-02
Date: 8 February 2024, 14:00-15:20
Location: Leipzig works, meeting room 2

Present: works management, production planning, quality, maintenance.

## 1. Output review

Output for January was 4 per cent below plan, driven by a two-day line stoppage in
week 3. Recovery is scheduled across February and March.

## 2. Product line

Kalthoff AG manufactures the Braxis hydraulic pump. The review confirmed that the pump
line keeps its current two-shift pattern through the summer.

## 3. Personnel

Marta Cendrillo works at Kalthoff AG and takes over production planning from the end
of March. Handover sessions are booked for the last week of the month.

## 4. Maintenance

The planned overhaul of the test bench moves from April to June to avoid the peak
call-off window.

Next review: 14 March 2024.
