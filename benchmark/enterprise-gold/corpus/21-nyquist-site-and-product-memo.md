# Memo - site and product line

Nyquist Sensors Oy
Ref: NS-MEM-2024-02
Date: 23 January 2024
To: sales, engineering
From: the works office

## 1. Site

Nyquist Sensors Oy is based in Tampere. The clean room extension was handed over in
November and is in qualification until the end of February.

## 2. Product line

Nyquist Sensors Oy manufactures the Nadir 12 sensor array. The array replaces the
older eight-channel unit, which goes end-of-sale at the close of the year.

## 3. Engineering contact

Nils Ekstrand works at Nyquist Sensors Oy and owns the qualification plan for the
extension.
