NYQUIST SENSORS OY - SUPPLIER NOTICE

Notice no.: NS-SN-2023-009
Issued: 14 August 2023
To: logistics partners and their dispatch desks

1. PURPOSE

This notice records the current delivery arrangement and the revised packing
requirement effective from September.

2. DELIVERY ARRANGEMENT

Nyquist Sensors Oy supplies Tessaro Logistik AG with sensor assemblies for onward
distribution. The arrangement has run since 2021 and is unchanged in scope.

3. PACKING

From 1 September all pallets must carry the two-dimensional label in the top right
corner of the shipping document. Pallets without the label will be accepted but
flagged.

4. CONTACT

Yusuf Kandemir works at Tessaro Logistik AG and handles dispatch queries under this
arrangement.
