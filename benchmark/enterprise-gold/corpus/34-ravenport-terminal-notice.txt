RAVENPORT TERMINALS LTD - TERMINAL NOTICE

Notice no.: RT-TN-2024-013
Issued: 19 March 2024
To: shipping lines, agents, hauliers

1. LOCATION

Ravenport Terminals Ltd is based in Rotterdam. Gate 4 remains closed for resurfacing
until 12 April; use gate 2 for all reefer traffic in the meantime.

2. SERVICE ARRANGEMENT

Ravenport Terminals Ltd supplies Dalgren Maritime AS with terminal handling and
storage services under a five-year contract.

3. GATE HOURS

Standard gate hours are unchanged. Extended hours apply on the last two working days
of each month.

4. DOCUMENTATION

Pre-advice is mandatory for all reefer units. Units arriving without pre-advice will
be turned away at the gate.
