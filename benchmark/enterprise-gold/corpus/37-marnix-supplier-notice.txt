MARNIX TEXTIL BV - SUPPLIER NOTICE

Notice no.: MT-SN-2024-004
Issued: 8 February 2024
To: customers under running contracts

1. PURPOSE

This notice confirms the running delivery arrangement and announces the change of
weaving mill for two article groups.

2. ARRANGEMENT

Marnix Textil BV supplies Pelmar Robotics SA with technical fabric for protective
covers. Deliveries run against the annual contract signed in November.

3. CHANGE OF MILL

From April, article groups 300 and 340 are woven at the second mill. Article numbers,
specifications and packing units are unchanged.

4. SAMPLES

First-off samples from the second mill are available on request from the sales desk
and will be sent ahead of the changeover.

Sales desk
sales@marnix-textil.example

5. REGISTERED OFFICE

Marnix Textil BV is based in Eindhoven.
