# Board note - portfolio review

Ref: MS-BRD-2024-03
Date: 6 February 2024
Prepared for: Supervisory Board, agenda item 4
Status: Draft, not minuted

## Background

The board asked for a short history of the portfolio additions of the last five years,
with the integration status of each. Only the industrial side is covered here; the
coatings side is covered in a separate note.

## History

Meridian Systems AG acquired Norvik Tools GmbH in March 2021. The transaction closed
after a six-month review and the target has reported into the drives division since.
A second addition followed two years later and is covered in the coatings note.

## Finance function

Ilse Brandtner works at Meridian Systems AG and has led group finance since the 2021
reorganisation. She will present the integration cost run-out at the March session.

## Requested decision

None. This note is for information only.
