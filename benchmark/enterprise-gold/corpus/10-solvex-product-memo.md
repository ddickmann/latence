# Product memo - membrane line

Solvex Chemie GmbH
Ref: SX-PM-2023-14
Date: 27 April 2023
To: sales, technical service
From: product management

## 1. Purpose

To brief the field on the membrane line ahead of the trade fair season. This memo is
technical background only and is not a price communication.

## 2. Product

Solvex Chemie GmbH manufactures the Halox membrane. The membrane is produced on the
line commissioned in 2019 and has been through two revisions since.

## 3. Positioning

The membrane sits between the entry-level and the high-temperature ranges. Field teams
should not position it against the high-temperature range on price alone.

## 4. Samples

Sample requests go through technical service. Turnaround is five working days, longer
during the fair weeks.
