# Joint statement

Ref: MS-PR-2024-07
Date: 2 April 2024
Issued by: the parties named below
Embargo: none

## Statement

Meridian Systems AG partnered with Ferrix Automation BV on a joint development
programme for drive electronics. The programme runs for an initial thirty-six months.

## Scope

The programme covers shared test infrastructure and a common interface specification.
Commercial terms are not part of this statement.

## Governance

A steering group meets quarterly, chaired alternately by each party. The first meeting
is scheduled for May.

## Media

Media questions go to the respective press offices; neither party will comment on
volumes or financial terms.

Issued jointly
