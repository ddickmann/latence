# Site memo

Lorquin Metals SpA
Ref: LM-SITE-2024-06
Date: 11 March 2024
To: works council, department heads

## 1. Location

Lorquin Metals SpA is based in Genoa. The rolling line and the finishing shop are on
the same site; the laboratory sits two streets away.

## 2. Energy

Peak-load shifting continues over the summer months. Departments must book furnace
time through planning as usual.

## 3. Personnel

Elena Marchetti works at Lorquin Metals SpA and chairs the energy working group. The
group reports monthly to the site management.

## 4. Visitors

Visitor access to the finishing shop requires the short safety briefing. Bookings
close at noon on the preceding working day.
