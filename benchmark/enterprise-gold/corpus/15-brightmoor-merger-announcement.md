# Announcement

Brightmoor Analytics Ltd
Ref: BA-ANN-2023-08
Date: 21 September 2023
Circulation: staff and retained advisers

## The transaction

Brightmoor Analytics Ltd merged with Ellsworth Diagnostics Inc in September 2023. The
combination was structured as a merger of equals and both boards recommended it.

## Name and branding

The combined business keeps both names in the market for at least eighteen months. A
single brand decision will be taken after the integration review.

## Immediate effect on staff

None. Contracts of employment transfer unchanged and no consultation is required at
this stage.
