# Memo - partnership programme

Quintara Energy plc
Ref: QE-PP-2024-04
Date: 18 June 2024
To: procurement, engineering, legal
From: strategy office

## 1. Purpose

To record the scope of the partnership agreed in June and the internal owners for each
workstream.

## 2. Partnership

Quintara Energy plc partnered with Vantor Stahlwerke AG on low-carbon process heat.
The agreement covers a joint pilot at one site and a shared study programme.

## 3. Workstreams

| Workstream        | Internal owner   |
|-------------------|------------------|
| Pilot engineering | engineering      |
| Commercial terms  | procurement      |
| Compliance review | legal            |

## 4. Reporting

The strategy office reports progress monthly to the executive committee. The first
report is due in July.

## 5. Confidentiality

The agreement is confidential. Nothing in it may be shared with third parties without
prior written consent from legal.
