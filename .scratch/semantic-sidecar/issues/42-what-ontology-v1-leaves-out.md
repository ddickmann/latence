## Result (14 Nov)

The order taken: mentions from the estate's documents, then the SHACL and PROV-O exports, as the
brief of 14 Nov sets it. The other two parts are tickets of their own.

- **Mentions** (`estate/mentions.py`, `build --documents <folder>`): every place a document
  (`.txt`, `.md`) names a row the export holds (a master record by its name, a value of a value
  set by its spelling or another one the build holds) is a mention: document, digest, half-open
  span, text and row. Names are found whole and case-folded, the longest at a place winning; a
  name two rows carry is kept with both candidates and linked to neither; names shorter than three
  characters are not looked for. Rows of an object about persons are never in the export, so
  never found. The export holds the spans, not the documents (`mentions`, `meta.mentions`).
  Tests: `estate/tests/test_mentions.py` (3; red: the build took no documents).
- **SHACL** (`ontology.shacl.ttl`, `estate/standards.py`): one node shape per concept that is not
  a value set, targeting the Turtle file's class; attributes with their datatype, a personal
  column with `sh:maxCount 0`, relations with the class of their end and, where many-to-one, at
  most one value. What a person has not approved is written deactivated
  (`sh:deactivated true`). Tests (`estate/tests/test_standards.py`): the shapes are valid SHACL
  (pyshacl's check against SHACL's own shapes), use the Turtle file's IRIs, hold a data graph to
  the approved many-to-one relation (one dealer conforms, two do not), and every personal column
  may hold no value. Red: no file was written.
- **PROV-O** (`ontology.prov.ttl`): every concept, relation and measure is a `prov:Entity`
  generated by the build and attributed to the engine; an element read from the catalog is
  derived from its catalog object; a person's decision is an activity associated with a
  `prov:Person`, influencing the element. Test: the same file (red: no file).
- `pyshacl` is pinned in the prototype's `requirements.txt`, for its tests only.
